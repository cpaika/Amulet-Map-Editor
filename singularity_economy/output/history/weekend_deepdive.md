## 3. Portfolio Construction Notes

**Scenario-probability-weighted sizing.** The book prices three worlds: baseline (boom persists on schedule), reflexive-bust (circular financing unwinds), v2 (q-brake + Wright learning + commoditization — the house structural view). Weight sizing by lens-*invariance*, not lens-average: a position earns full size only if its sign survives all three lenses (POWL, 6501.T, 002472.SZ, MSFT, RHI, CHRW); sign-flippers (NVDA, TSM, BESI, CLS, all merchant power) are rentals sized to their v2 worst case, not their baseline E[up]. Suggested working weights ~40/25/35 across baseline/enhanced/v2 — but the operative rule is simpler: **no position whose thesis exists only in the baseline lens may exceed half-size.**

**Silicon as a dated cyclical, not a holding.** The model's clock — chips bind 2026-27, silicon margins peak ~2029, rents normalized by 2033 — dictates: fully invested now through the 2026-27 bind (Rubin, HBM4, TSMC 2027 price hikes are pre-announced catalysts), **begin scaling down into the 2028-29 margin peak, not after**, flat merchant silicon by 2030-31. The red team converted the calendar into indicators: MU/Hynix exit on Samsung wpm confirmation and first sequential DRAM contract-price decline; BESI rebuild only ~25x; ASML is the one silicon name to still own in 2030.

**Reflexive-bust hedges.** (a) The AVGO-long/MRVL-short pair (once the Trainium facts are verified) is the sleeve's internal hedge — it monetizes commoditization without needing the bust. (b) RHI/CHRW/HUBS shorts are structurally bust-correlated. (c) Vendor-financing watch items (NVDA investee round-tripping, AVGO's OpenAI counterparty, CLS's equity raise) are the early-warning channel — CLS at tracker size is itself the canary. (d) Cheap 2028 bust convexity, if wanted, is EQIX puts at the deceleration point — not now (no 2026-27 catalyst, pure carry loss).

**Taiwan tail.** Un-hedgeable inside the silicon sleeve: in the invasion branch the Silicon pool's scarcity margin *rises* while TSM equity is destroyed. Therefore: cap TSM below AVGO, treat its discount as tail compensation, and recognize the natural cross-hedges are the non-Taiwan capacity holders (MU, Samsung-exposure via the Hynix pair, US/JP grid names whose backlogs would extend in a rebuild scenario). Any index-level cross-strait hedge is bought vol, budgeted separately.

**Sleeve falsifiers (model signposts).**
- **Grid/power longs:** capacity-glut ratio >1.15 in the model's power module, or power-margin rollover (Hitachi Energy <13%, GEV Electrification margin, POWL two-quarter book-to-bill <1.2x, reservation-to-firm conversion stalling) — that is the Wright-learning supply response arriving early; trim the whole sleeve, not just the name that printed it.
- **Silicon:** glut >1.15 in silicon-equivalent (Samsung HBM capacity confirmation, blended DRAM contract price sequential decline) fires the memory exits; TSMC pricing power failing to stick in 2027 fires the rest.
- **Displacement shorts:** the thesis *requires* wage-bill inflections 2028-31 — if perm placement (RHI), pays-per-control (ADP), and organic cRPO (WDAY) are all still positive by mid-2028, the displacement dating is wrong and the short complex unwinds; conversely staffing revenue re-acceleration for two quarters is the sector-level stop.
- **Robot components:** humanoid unit shipments vs the model's ramp; Shuanghuan/JL MAG order books are the tell. Theme-multiple compression in the China basket without unit deterioration is noise; unit misses are signal.
- **Merchant power:** 2028 hedge-book rolloff meeting spark-spread compression = the sunset trigger already pre-committed on VST.
