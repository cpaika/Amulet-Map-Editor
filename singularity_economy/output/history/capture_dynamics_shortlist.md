## Rank 1 — Per-company capital structure: net debt with rolling refinancing cost (equity = EV - net debt)
*Sources: Proposals 5 (credit-microstructure) + 10 (valuation-realism) — two independent angles converged on the same spec; weekend_deepdive.md:145 (section 5 item 14: 'unmodeled 4x leverage')*
**Why load-bearing:** VERIFIED ABSENT: grep for net_debt/interest/coupon in valuation.rs and companies.rs returns nothing; Company struct (valuation.rs:107-131) has only ticker/mcap_b/ntm_earnings_b/pools/pool_beta/share_drift/terminal_multiple/stance/capture/taiwan_fab_exposure. evaluate() (valuation.rs:286-362) compares unlevered earnings PV straight to mcap_b. The deep dive PROVED this matters (item 14: NRG 4x leverage invisible; NRG sizing capped at 50-60% by hand because the model cannot see it). The model's own B11 long_rate rises to ~5.4% in high-transfer scenarios but reprices no one's interest expense — levered names look rate-immune exactly where the model raises rates. Cheapest satellite relative to how many existing book numbers it corrects; zero sim-core changes.
**Mechanism:** STATE: two new Company fields, net_debt_b: f64 (default 0.0) and debt_coupon_0: f64 (default 0.0), populated via the load_financials JSON override path (companies.rs:181-202) from the task-#43 financials refresh (NRG ~4x, VST post-hedge-rolloff, TLN, EQIX, CBZ, TECK). Global param leverage_gain (gated 0.0). UPDATE: inside the earnings_and_rent year loop (valuation.rs:155-200, which already iterates YearState), maintain per-name coupon_t += 0.15 * (s.long_rate + credit_spread_per_turn * turns - coupon_t) — the exact sovereign-roll pattern from macrofin.rs:159 — and subtract leverage_gain * coupon_t * net_debt_b from each year's earnings BEFORE the terminal slice (so terminal_multiple capitalizes post-interest earnings). In evaluate() (valuation.rs:337-341), upside = (fair_PV) / mcap_b - 1 unchanged, but fair_PV is now the levered-equity PV; floor deeply negative levered paths at a distress stub (0.1x revenue claim) rather than symmetric negative PV. COUPLING POINTS: valuation.rs earnings_and_rent loop (reads s.long_rate, already in YearState — currently only consumed by scenario_discount at valuation.rs:268 and terminal_discount at :281); companies.rs universe rows + load_financials keys; no lib.rs changes. Composes with rank-2 (sector-level debt service) without double-counting: pools are operating profits, not entity P&Ls.
**Params/stocks:** Params: leverage_gain (0.0 gate — at 0 the interest subtraction and coupon roll are skipped entirely, byte-identical book), refi_roll (0.15), credit_spread_per_turn (~40bp per turn of net_debt/EBITDA), distress_floor_mult (0.1). Per-name data: net_debt_b (0.0 default), debt_coupon_0. New state: per-name coupon path (local to earnings_and_rent, no persistent stock).
**Lock:** Three locks: (1) golden-book lock — leverage_gain=0.0 OR all net_debt_b=0.0 reproduces the shipped book bit-for-bit; (2) dispersion lock — with NRG's real net debt on, its per-scenario upside spread (best minus worst) strictly WIDENS vs the unlevered run, and the widening is monotone in net_debt_b; (3) rate-sign lock — rank correlation between per-scenario mean long_rate and the levered name's upside becomes strictly more negative than the same name unlevered (levered names are short duration in exactly the scenarios where B11 raises rates), while an all-equity name in the same pool is bit-identical across both runs.

## Rank 2 — AI-sector debt service, coupon roll, and the refinancing wall
*Sources: Proposal 4 (credit-microstructure); deep-dive leverage blindness at the sector level; macrofin.rs:96-160 supplies the proven pattern*
**Why load-bearing:** VERIFIED ABSENT: sector_debt accrues at lib.rs:1399-1400 (sector_debt = sector_debt * p.debt_amortization + ai_capex * (1 - internal_funding_share)) with no coupon, no maturity structure; its only effect is the leverage quantity signal in credit_denom (lib.rs:1223, 1240-1248). The sovereign gets a rolling coupon (macrofin.rs:159-160) but the AI sector finances for free forever in a 5.4% world. The q-governor numerator (lib.rs:1191, prev.profits.ai_services) is pre-interest, so the profitability brake fires too late — this satellite moves the model's bust CLOCK, which the trade book's 'scale down into 2028-29' schedule and the EQIX-put timing both key on. Also feeds the queued Kalecki closure for free (interest paid is income not respent). Not in the philosophy shortlist; no open task covers it.
**Mechanism:** STATE: new f64 ai_coupon (init long_rate_2026 + ai_spread, alongside sector_debt in the step loop). UPDATE each year: roll share = (1 - p.debt_amortization) + new-issuance fraction; ai_coupon += roll * (long_rate_prev + ai_spread + spread_widening - ai_coupon), where spread_widening reuses the SAME terms already summed into credit_denom at lib.rs:1240-1248 (soc_credit + macro_credit + gfx.spread + bfx.spread - es_dev*2) so a Minsky year reprices the roll, not the legacy stock. interest = ai_coupon * sector_debt; debit ai_interest_gain * interest from prev-year ai_services profit where the q-governor reads it (lib.rs:1191 let ai_profit = prev.profits.ai_services - gated interest) — the hurdle at lib.rs:1201 already contains prev.long_rate, closing the loop on the numerator side it currently ignores. REFI WALL: when credit_mult < refi_floor, the maturing share that cannot roll amortizes from capex: ai_capex -= ((1 - credit_mult/refi_floor).clamp(0,1)) * (1 - p.debt_amortization) * sector_debt, applied after the Liebig min at lib.rs:1377-1391, clamped at 0. COUPLING POINTS: lib.rs:1399-1400 (state home), lib.rs:1189-1206 (q numerator), lib.rs:1240-1249 (spread reuse), macrofin long_rate via prev YearState (B11 now transmits to AI-sector cash flow, not just credit quantity and the DCF discount).
**Params/stocks:** Params: ai_interest_gain (0.0 gate — interest debit and wall both gain-multiplied, byte-identical at 0), ai_spread (0.015 over long_rate), refi_floor (0.6 on credit_mult), roll fraction implied by existing debt_amortization. New stock: ai_coupon.
**Lock:** Timing lock: with q_governor_gain=1.0 and ai_interest_gain on, q crosses below 1 strictly EARLIER and the peak-ai_capex year moves earlier, monotone in ai_spread, while 2026-2028 paths are nearly unchanged (debt stock still small — a timing satellite, not a level rescale). Wall lock: spine (equity_sentiment_gain=1.0) + wall on gives a strictly lower trough ai_capex than spine-only AND sector_debt falls faster in the bust year (procyclical deleveraging). Gate lock: 0.0 byte-identical against golden CSVs.

## Rank 3 — Contract-book split on the electricity pool: contracted vs merchant with tenor and repricing lag
*Sources: Proposals 6 (contracts-backlogs) + 3b (credit-microstructure) — cross-angle convergence; weekend_deepdive.md:145 (item 14: 'contract duration and counterparty quality are invisible; CEG v2 -38% too harsh'); gated_dynamics_menu.md:12 claims the spine 'favors contracted CEG over merchant VST/NRG' — currently true only in prose, not code*
**Why load-bearing:** VERIFIED ABSENT: pools.electricity = used_power * 8760 * electricity_price / 1e6 (lib.rs:1783) with ONE margin, electricity_margin = (0.30 + 0.5*(price/normal - 1)).clamp(0,0.6) (lib.rs:1800-1803) — all revenue is spot. No contract state anywhere (grep 'contract_' in src/: zero hits). The deep dive PROVED this is the book's flagged sign-quality problem: CEG's 10-20yr PPAs (and TLN's $18B AWS PPA) are priced as evaporating merchant rent; the live 'conditional trim' decision on CEG (deepdive line 65) rides on it; VST's 2028 hedge-rolloff sunset is a hand rule the model could emit. The gated power_glut_price_gain can only crash contracted and merchant indiscriminately.
**Mechanism:** STATE: contract_price_rel (init 1.0, price relative to electricity_price_normal) and contract_cover_share as a param. UPDATE: each year a fraction (1/contract_tenor_years + new_contract_share * additions/stock) of the book reprices at the current spot ratio: contract_price_rel += roll_frac * (spot_rel - contract_price_rel), clamped [0.4, 3.0]. Effective pool price = (1 - cover) * spot + cover * contract_price_rel * normal, applied where electricity_price is consumed at lib.rs:1783; margin computed twice — merchant slice at the live spot margin (lib.rs:1800-1803 expression on spot), contracted slice with the same formula on contract_price_rel. SPLIT Profits.electricity into electricity_merchant and electricity_contracted sub-flows (Profits struct near lib.rs:1804-1808). VALUATION: add EmergingPool::ElectricityContracted so companies.rs maps CEG (and the queued TLN row) onto the contracted slice while VST/NRG stay merchant; VST's per-company declining cover schedule expresses the 2028 hedge rolloff. COUPLING POINTS: lib.rs:1783 (pool revenue blend), lib.rs:1800-1808 (margin split into Profits), valuation.rs EmergingPool enum + capture loop (valuation.rs:196-200), companies.rs power rows.
**Params/stocks:** Params: contract_cover_share (0.0 gate — blend collapses to spot exactly, byte-identical), contract_tenor_years (12), new_contract_share (0.5). New stock: contract_price_rel. New outputs: profits.electricity_merchant / electricity_contracted.
**Lock:** Glut lock: cover=0.6 + power_glut_price_gain=1.0 in a demand-fizzle run cuts merchant electricity profit ~45-50% while the contracted slice declines only at ~1/tenor per year. Symmetry lock: in the 2028-31 scarcity peak the contracted slice UNDER-earns spot (the lag cuts both ways — why CEG lagged VST in the boom); assert contracted < merchant margin in peak years and the ordering flips in glut years. Gate lock: cover=0.0 byte-identical.

COUPLING POINTS: lib.rs:1399-1400, lib.rs:1210-1214, lib.rs:1805, the ai_ig issuance feed into macrofin, valuation.rs silicon-weighted names. **Mechanism:** STATE: equip_backlog_gw plus a slow conversion-capacity stock equip_capacity_gwpy (init ~35, grows at equip_capacity_growth, modestly accelerated by sustained excess power_margin — lagging a boom by construction). UPDATE: inflow = orders from power_orders() (lib.rs:1297-1300); deliveries = min(backlog, capacity). **Params/stocks:** Params: equip_backlog_gain (0.0 gate), equip_conversion_capacity_2026 (35 GW/yr), equip_capacity_growth (0.12), equip_cancel_frac (0.3). New stocks: equip_backlog_gw, equip_capacity_gwpy. New observables: book_to_bill, implied_lead_time.

## Rank 6 — Endogenous algorithmic progress: research capability feeds algo_eff (software-only RSI stress test)

The model's headline claim — 'the singularity is gated by physical constraints' — is therefore ASSUMED, not derived. **Mechanism:** STATE: none persistent beyond the existing algo_eff — a gated blend at the single growth expression. UPDATE: R_t = prev.ai_hew_m * research_alloc_const (0.05); g_endog = 1 + algo_base_step + rsi_gain * ln(1 + R_t / R_2026); g_eff - 1 = ((1 - endog_algo_gain) * (g_calendar - 1) + endog_algo_gain * (g_endog - 1)) * headroom, where g_calendar and headroom are the existing lib.rs:1006-1015 expressions verbatim. R_2026 captured in year 1 so the blend is self-normalizing. NEW OUTPUT: endogenous_singularity_year = first year ai_hew crosses a threshold, reported beside the exogenous p.singularity_year. COUPLING POINTS: lib.rs:1005-1016 (sole modification), reads prev YearState ai_hew_m; downstream free through ai_hew (:1081), disp_target, ai_services (:1766), and B12's governance_throttle (lib.rs:1409-1412) — which now throttles a genuine flywheel instead of a schedule, making compute-governance scenarios bite capability TIMING, not just capex.
**Params/stocks:** Params: endog_algo_gain (0.0 gate — blend collapses to the calendar path exactly, byte-identical), rsi_gain (0.5), algo_base_step (0.15), research_alloc_const (0.05). No new stocks.
**Lock:** Embargo lock: at gain 0, assert algo_eff paths are IDENTICAL across baseline and taiwan_shock (the current provable blindness); with gain on, taiwan_shock reaches any given algo_eff level strictly LATER and fast_takeoff strictly EARLIER, monotone in rsi_gain. Software-only lock: a compute-frozen counterfactual (chip caps forced to 0 post-2028) still shows positive but slower algo growth — quantifying exactly how much takeoff survives a physical embargo, which is the headline stress test. Gate lock: 0.0 byte-identical.
